| 2026-11-15 | MarsRon | Claude Code (Opus 5.5) | Found the cause: the south-pole terrain and the north cap were drawn at their real heights right to their edge, while the globe and the level-of-detail patch meet them at the sphere, so a step (ground above the sphere) or a crack (ground below it) showed along 80 degrees. Added `capWeight` and `drawnDemHeight` to `web/src/scene/terrain.ts`: a polar mesh's shape eases to the sphere over the last degree before the globe's opening (smoothstep), the same idea as the patch's own faded edge. Colours and lighting still come from the full heights, so the band keeps its craters' shading. `restretchTerrain` and the pins and camera target (`drawnHeight` in `scene/index.ts`) use the eased heights. New test in `terrain.test.ts`; two north-cap tests in `globe.test.ts` now expect the eased heights.
