# AI use: issue #196, the mission playback's speed buttons

| When | Who | Tool | What the AI did | What a person checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-15 | Leon | Claude Code (Opus 5.5) | Filed the issue after measuring both pages in headless Chromium (2D mission card and 3D simulation, 8 points through the mission). 1 min/s worked everywhere, but 1 h/s and 6 h/s played at Auto's speed near every burn. Then fixed it on branch `issue-196-manual-speed`. Cause: `web/src/ui/mission/pace.ts` `regions()` gave every mode Auto's 60 s/s windows of ±15 min round TLI, LOI and DOI. A probe of the whole mission (the test plan, 60 frames a second) showed those windows plus the two 30 s/s burns took about 140 of the 160 to 260 real seconds in every manual mode, and that a steeper ramp barely helped (6 h/s at its speed 7 % of the time at the current ramp, 10 % at a ramp almost seven times steeper). The fix: only Auto adds the windows round TLI, LOI and DOI and the arrival window. A speed button keeps the ascent and the powered descent at 30 s/s (which predates #105, and the page says so), and every change stays gradual (same `RAMP`). New `heldText()` says what plays and why while a manual speed isn't reached ("now 30 s/s during the ascent", "now 5 min/s, slowing for the powered descent", "speeding up after the ascent", "speeding up" or "slowing down" while it glides). Both pages use it instead of their own "now …" lines. The help text on the card says Auto slows round TLI, LOI and DOI. Tests in `pace.test.ts`: the burn test covers every mode (ascent and powered descent at most 30 s/s), with Auto still slow at LOI and DOI; new tests check that 1 h/s plays TLI, LOI and DOI at 1 h/s, that 1 h/s and 6 h/s reach their speed in the long stages (6 h/s plays the mission in under half of Auto's time), and the `heldText` wording. With the old windows put back, the two new speed tests fail. After the fix the probe measured: 1 h/s at its speed all of the coast and lunar orbit (was 66 % and 39 %), 6 h/s 96 % and 74 % (was 21 % and 6 %); Auto unchanged (216.8 s). Measured both pages again in the browser: at 1.2 % 1 h/s and 6 h/s now play at 3603 and 21087 s/s (were 693 and 627), and at 97 % 1 h/s at 2896 (was 1125); where a button is still held, next to the two burns, the note says why. Checks: `npm test` 785 of 785, `npm run build` passes, `npm run test:page` 21 of 21. | Leon asked for the issue and the fix. P5 agreed to the design change (a speed button passes TLI, LOI and DOI at its own speed; only Auto slows for them), recorded in `docs/decisions.md`. Leon tried the speed buttons in the browser on the pull request's preview on 15 Nov 2026 |
