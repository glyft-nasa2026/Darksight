| How the pad coordinates are checked | All nineteen pads in `core/pads.ts` (the twelve above and the brief's seven) are checked against their pages by a Playwright script (headless Chromium, `@playwright/test` 1.65.1) that an AI agent ran on 15 Nov 2026, one page at a time. It lists every coordinate on each page with its context (title, infobox, table row, list item, section), and where a page gives only the spaceport it follows the pad's own article, the raw wikitext of the page's map template, or the map's Wikidata item. More than 0.01° off in latitude or longitude is wrong, 0.002° to 0.01° close, under 0.002° a match. Every pad matches except Taiyuan LC-9, which held the page title's coordinate and now holds LC-9's. The brief's seven match their rows above exactly; Rocket Lab's is LC-1 as a whole, with pads A and B within 0.0007°. The full table, for a person to confirm, is in pull request #185 | - | - |
