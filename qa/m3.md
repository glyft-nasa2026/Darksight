## 60-second test

Someone outside the team who hasn't seen the site. No instructions apart from the question.

1. Open https://darksight.space on a laptop (it opens on the crater rim as Live against the crater
   floor as A). Hand it over.
2. Ask, word for word: "Which of these two sites gets more sunlight this month?" Start the timer.
3. Stop the timer at their answer. The correct answer is the rim (Live). Write down where they
   hesitated and what they clicked, without helping.
